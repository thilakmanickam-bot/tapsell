Features

Everything a chat seller needs to close the sale

Built for one-person shops that take orders on WhatsApp: boutiques, home bakers, jewellery and beauty sellers.

Replies in their language

English, Tamil and Tanglish at launch, matched to how each customer writes. Hindi and Malay next.

One-tap approval

Every draft waits for you. Edit it, send it or discard it. Nothing goes out automatically.

Knows your stock

Drafts use your product list, prices and stock, so customers get accurate answers.

Orders in Google Sheets

Each confirmed order becomes a row in your own sheet, with status from waiting to paid.

Your payment link

Replies include your Razorpay link or UPI QR. Money goes straight to your account.

Private by design

Tapsell holds chat text only until you reply, or 24 hours at most, and never saves it to disk or logs.

FAQ

Questions sellers ask

Does Tapsell send messages without me?

No. Every reply is a draft until you approve it. You can edit or discard any draft.

Do you store my customers' chats?

Tapsell does not. Messages are shown to you and cleared from Tapsell when you reply or after 24 hours, and are never saved to our database, logs or backups. To draft a reply, the message text is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, which deletes it within 30 days by default. Details are in our privacy policy.

Does Tapsell handle payments?

No. Replies include your own Razorpay link or UPI QR, and money goes straight to you. You mark an order paid once you see it.

What do I need to start?

A WhatsApp Business number, a Google account for the order sheet, and your product list with prices and stock.

Does it work with Instagram?

Instagram direct messages are planned shortly after launch. The pilot starts with WhatsApp.
