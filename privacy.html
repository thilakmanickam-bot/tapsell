Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · Draft for the waitlist and pilot · Version 3

In short: Tapsell never stores your customers' chats. Messages are shown to you and cleared from Tapsell when you reply or after 24 hours at most. Orders go to your own Google Sheet, and payments go straight to you.

1. Who we are and how to reach us

Tapsell is a service that drafts WhatsApp replies for small sellers. It is a product of TechByT, operated from Singapore ("we", "us"). TechByT's registration number, registered address and business email will be added here once registration is complete.

Data protection contact: TechByT's founder acts as data protection officer and grievance contact. WhatsApp +65 8869 8660. A business email will be added here before paid sign-ups open.

2. Joining the waitlist

When you join the waitlist, the page prepares a WhatsApp message and you choose whether to send it. Sending it is your consent for us to use the details below for the purposes below. If you send it, we receive:

  • your name, business name, country and city
  • your product category and how many customer messages you get a day
  • where your orders come in and which languages your customers use
  • your WhatsApp number and profile name, which WhatsApp shares with us when you message us

Why: to contact you about Tapsell, choose pilot sellers and plan the product. We do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

How long: we delete them 12 months after our last contact with you, or sooner if you ask.

Changing your mind: send STOP to the same WhatsApp number and we will remove you from the waitlist and delete your details.

3. This website

This page is hosted on Anthropic's claude.ai platform, which may record standard visit information such as your IP address and browser under Anthropic's own privacy terms. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google when it opens. The page itself does not save what you type into the form and does not use our own cookies or analytics.

5. Your customers' messages

  • Not stored by Tapsell. Incoming messages are held in short-term memory only, so they can be shown to you and a reply drafted. They are cleared from Tapsell when you reply or after 24 hours, whichever comes first. They are not written to our database, logs, error reports, backups or analytics.
  • Drafting. To draft a reply, the message text, your product list and your settings are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude API). Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days by default. It may keep them for up to 2 years if they are flagged as breaking its usage policy, or longer where the law requires. See Anthropic's retention page.
  • WhatsApp. Messages travel through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and are handled under Meta's terms.
  • Orders. When you approve an order, its details (such as customer name, phone, items and address) are written to your own Google Sheet, not to Tapsell's database. Tapsell can open only the sheet you choose for orders, not the rest of your Google Drive. Google's terms apply to your sheet.
  • Nothing is sent without you. A reply leaves only after you approve it.

Who is responsible. For your customers' information, you, the seller, decide what is collected and why (you are the data fiduciary or organisation). Tapsell processes it only on your instructions, to show messages, draft replies and record orders (Tapsell is your data processor or data intermediary). You are responsible for telling your customers how you use their details. Our pilot terms set out how we handle your customers' information.

6. Payments

Tapsell does not process or hold your customers' payments. Replies include your own payment link or UPI QR. When you pay for Tapsell, our payment provider (Razorpay) processes your payment and we receive only a payment record, not your card or bank details.

7. Service providers and overseas transfers

We use a small number of providers to run Tapsell: cloud hosting and database, Anthropic (AI drafting), Meta (WhatsApp), Google (your order sheet, when you connect it) and Razorpay (subscription payments). Data may be processed in India, Singapore, the United States and other countries where these providers operate. When data leaves the country it was collected in, we rely on providers whose contracts and terms give it a standard of protection comparable to the law that applies to you.

8. Security and breaches

Connection keys are encrypted, each seller can see only their own data, access is limited to people who need it to run the service, and we check before every release that no message text reaches our storage or logs. No system is perfectly secure.

If a breach affects personal data, we will act to limit the harm and notify as the law requires: for India, the affected people and the Data Protection Board straight away, with a detailed report to the Board within 72 hours; for Singapore, the Personal Data Protection Commission within 3 calendar days of assessing that a breach must be reported, and affected people where significant harm is likely.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see a summary of the details we hold about you, correct or update them, delete them, or withdraw your consent at any time. In India you can also nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot act. Message us on WhatsApp at +65 8869 8660. We will confirm your request and reply within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India or Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission, depending on where you live.
